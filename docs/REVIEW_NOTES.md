# Notes for the native-speaker reviewers

Points the content authors were unsure about. Check them together with `CONTENT_REVIEW.md`.

## cs-en (English)
- Unit 1: hello = „dobrý den“, hi = „ahoj“, bye = „čau“ (note: „ahoj“ also works), excuse me =
  „s dovolením“, sorry = „promiňte; omlouvám se“ — chosen so tests have one clear answer.
- "Good afternoon" = „dobré odpoledne“ (rare in Czech; explained in a note).
- „Jmenuji se“ used rather than colloquial „Jmenuju se“.
- Syllabus: numbers split 1–9 (U2) / 10–100 (U3); months split in U12; wh-words listed as words only in U11.

## cs-de (German)
- Pronunciation hints in Czech spelling are approximate (least sure: Wiedersehen ≈ „vídrzéen“).
- Tschüss = „čau (na rozloučenou)“, Hallo = „ahoj“ (kept distinct for tests).
- „Guten Morgen“: note says until about 10 a.m. (regional).
- Syllabus: „null“ only in U19, „hundert“ in U17, months with articles (der Januar).

## cs-ro (Romanian)
- Pronunciation hints approximate: ă ≈ „ehm“, î compared with Russian ы, „mulcumesk“, „če maj fač“.
- „Pa“ glossed „pa; čau (při loučení)“; „Bună dimineața“ note „asi do deseti dopoledne“.
- „Mersi“ accepted as a typed answer but not taught as its own word.
