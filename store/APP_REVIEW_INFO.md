2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Elenka is a free language-learning app for Czech speakers (English, German and Romanian courses, levels A1–B1) and for Romanian speakers learning Czech. It was built for our own family: older learners and complete beginners who find mainstream apps too fast, too small or too pushy. It offers short lessons with clear explanations in the learner's own language, large text and buttons, spaced-repetition flashcards, and no ads, accounts, tracking or purchases. Progress is stored only on the device.

3. HOW TO USE THE MAIN FEATURES
No login or setup is needed. On first launch, choose your native language and the language to learn, then tap "Začneme!" (Let's start). The app opens on the home screen "Domů" (Home): tap the big "Začít" (Start) button to begin the first lesson and answer the exercises. The whole course is on the "Kurz" (Course) tab, words to repeat under "Opakování" (Review), and statistics under the "Pokrok" (Progress) tile on the home screen. "Sdílet aplikaci" (Share the app) opens the system share sheet with a link to the app's web page. "Nastavení" (Settings) contains the languages, reading speed and voice, the microphone exercises switch and a backup of progress.
Optional "Řekněte nahlas" (Say it out loud) exercises use the microphone; they can be turned off in Nastavení > "Cvičení s mikrofonem".
Optional family feature: an AI conversation partner ("Konverzace") is hidden until a family access code is entered in Nastavení > Konverzace s AI > Rodinný kód. To test it, enter the code given at the end of these notes, tap "Uložit kód", finish all lessons of any unit, then tap "Konverzace" at the bottom of that unit.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Apple's built-in text-to-speech (on device) reads words and sentences aloud.
- Apple's speech recognition (SFSpeechRecognizer) checks pronunciation in the optional speaking exercises.
- The optional family AI conversation uses Anthropic's Claude API through our own small server on Cloudflare Workers. Messages are sent only when the user writes in the conversation and are not linked to any identity.
- The privacy policy is hosted on GitHub Pages.
There are no analytics, advertising, authentication or payment services.

5. REGIONS
The app works the same in all regions; all course content is bundled in the app and works offline.
